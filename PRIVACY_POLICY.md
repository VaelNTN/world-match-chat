## English (Google Play Store Review)
WorldMatch Chat is an 18+ anonymous global 1-on-1 chat application with gender and language matching.
- **Local storage:** Your nickname, selected gender preference, selected languages, country flag, match history, and blocked user IDs are stored locally on your device (`localStorage`).
- **Temporary Matchmaking & Chat Messages:** When connecting to the online matchmaking API, only your randomly generated `deviceId`, chosen nickname, gender selection, country flag, language codes, and temporary chat messages are processed in the database. Inactive queue records expire automatically after 20 seconds.
- **Safety & Moderation:** Users can leave any chat immediately, switch to the next match, or block a partner locally at any time. We do not collect real names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise GPS locations, or advertising IDs.
- **Data deletion:** Uninstall the app or clear storage to erase all local data.

## Nederlands
WorldMatch Chat is een anonieme wereldwijde 18+ chat-app op basis van gekozen geslacht en talen. Er worden geen echte namen, e-mailadressen, telefoonnummers of GPS-locaties opgeslagen. Je kunt elk gesprek direct verlaten of een gebruiker blokkeren.
